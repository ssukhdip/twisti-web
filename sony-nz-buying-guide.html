Sony in NZ: the complete buying guide

Headphones, cameras, TVs and PlayStation — how Sony's lineup breaks down and where the real savings are in New Zealand.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026

Sony's range in NZ, at a glance

  • Headphones — the WH-1000X noise-cancelling series is Sony's flagship line, alongside cheaper WH-CH and true wireless earbud options.
  • PlayStation — consoles, controllers and accessories, sold identically across every major NZ electronics retailer.
  • TVs — Bravia OLED and LED ranges, positioned as a premium alternative to Samsung and LG with strong picture processing.
  • Cameras — the Alpha mirrorless range, typically sold through specialist camera retailers as much as general electronics stores.

Where prices actually differ

Sony headphones and TVs see genuine price variation between JB Hi-Fi, Noel Leeming, PB Tech and Harvey Norman — it's worth comparing before buying rather than assuming parity. PlayStation console and first-party accessory pricing tends to track much closer together across retailers, similar to other console hardware.

When Sony gear actually goes on sale

Sony's flagship headphones (the WH-1000X series) see their deepest discounts once a new generation launches and the outgoing model gets cleared — historically a 20-30% drop is common at that point. TVs follow the same model-year cycle as other TV brands, with the best pricing on outgoing models in the September-October window before the new range lands. PlayStation console bundles are strongest around Black Friday and Boxing Day.

The previous-generation headphones are usually the smartest buy. Sony's noise-cancelling improvements between generations are real but incremental — buying the outgoing WH-1000X model once the new one launches typically gets you 90% of the performance for notably less money.

The bottom line

For headphones and TVs, compare across retailers and consider buying the outgoing generation once a new model launches — for PlayStation hardware, focus on bundle value during sale events instead, since the console price itself rarely moves.
