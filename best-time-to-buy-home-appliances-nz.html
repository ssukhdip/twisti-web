Best Time to Buy Home Appliances in NZ

Fridges, washing machines, and dishwashers don't discount randomly — retailers follow a predictable annual pattern once you know where to look.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
How we picked these: Timing patterns are based on historical pricing behaviour from major NZ appliance retailers and typical new-model release cycles from manufacturers.

The two biggest sale windows

Boxing Day through late January is the single biggest appliance discount period in NZ, as retailers like Harvey Norman, Noel Leeming, and The Warehouse clear stock ahead of new financial year ranges and compete hard for post-Christmas spending. Discounts on fridges and laundry appliances of 15–25% off ticketed price are common during this window, and it's also when older model-year stock gets pushed out to make room for new releases.

The second major window is Black Friday/Cyber Monday in late November, which has grown significantly in NZ over the past several years and now rivals Boxing Day for appliance deals specifically — though stock availability on specific models can be more limited than the January clearance period.

Why timing around model releases matters

Most major appliance brands — Fisher & Paykel, Samsung, LG, Bosch — refresh their fridge and laundry ranges on a roughly annual cycle, often with new models landing in NZ showrooms around March to May. That means the outgoing model year often sees its steepest discounting in the couple of months right before the new range arrives — sometimes a bigger saving than waiting for a general sale event, especially if you don't need the newest features.

Category-specific patterns

  • Fridges and freezers — best discounts around Boxing Day and again in autumn as new-season models arrive; avoid buying right before Christmas when demand (and prices) peak.
  • Washing machines and dryers — similar pattern to fridges, with an additional smaller dip around Father's Day (early September) when some retailers run laundry-specific promotions.
  • Dishwashers — tend to track general appliance sale cycles rather than having their own distinct pattern; Boxing Day and Black Friday are the reliable windows.
  • Small kitchen appliances (air fryers, kettles, coffee machines) — heavily discounted around Mother's Day (May) and Christmas gift season, since they're common gift purchases.

Don't ignore end-of-financial-year clearances

Many NZ retailers also run clearance pricing around March, tied to end-of-financial-year stock clearing rather than a specific calendar sale event — this is less predictable and less heavily advertised than Boxing Day, but can turn up genuine discounts on floor-stock or ex-display models if you're willing to ask in-store.

Practical buying tips

  • Avoid buying in an emergency if you can help it — if your current appliance is failing but still limping along, waiting a few weeks for the next sale window can be worth hundreds of dollars.
  • Ask about ex-display or floor models — these carry the same warranty in most cases but can be discounted 10–20% simply for cosmetic wear or because a new model has replaced them on the showroom floor.
  • Compare the same model across retailers before assuming a "sale" price is actually the best price available — ticketed discounts don't always beat a competitor's everyday price.
