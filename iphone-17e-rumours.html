iPhone 17e Rumours: Everything We Know So Far

Update: the iPhone 17e is no longer a rumour. Apple announced it on 2 March 2026 and it went on sale 11 March 2026. This page has been updated to show what leakers got right, what they got wrong, and what the phone actually is now that it's shipping.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Why we're labelling this so carefully: Because this page started life as a pre-launch rumour tracker, we're keeping the confirmed/reported/opinion structure so you can see exactly which pre-launch reports turned out to be accurate, rather than quietly rewriting history now the phone is out.

What's confirmed

Confirmed

The iPhone 17e starts at USD $599, with a base storage bump to 256GB (double the previous 16e's base storage) and a 512GB option at USD $799. It launched in white, black, and a new pink colour option.

Confirmed

It uses Apple's A19 chip (6-core CPU, 4-core GPU, 16-core Neural Engine), Apple's first cellular modem generation continuing from the 16e, and adds MagSafe wireless charging for the first time on an "e" model.

Confirmed

Despite persistent pre-launch rumours of a Dynamic Island, Apple kept the traditional notch and an overall design very close to the iPhone 16e.

What's reported

Reported

Ahead of launch, Bloomberg's Mark Gurman and analyst Ming-Chi Kuo were the most consistently cited sources on iPhone "e" model timing and Apple's broader strategy of releasing budget models earlier in the year and premium models later — a pattern Kuo's supply-chain notes described as becoming the new norm for 2025-2027 releases.

Reported

Some pre-launch coverage speculated the 17e's pricing strategy was designed to keep the eventual iPhone 18 Pro price unchanged — reported as an industry read on Apple's margin strategy rather than anything Apple confirmed directly.

Twisti's opinion

Opinion

The rumour mill got the broad strokes right on this one — chip, storage bump, and rough price were all close to what shipped — but the Dynamic Island speculation was a reminder that "it would make sense for Apple to do this" isn't the same as a confirmed leak. If you're shopping in NZ now, the 17e is a genuinely solid mid-range pick if you don't need Pro camera features, but check current NZ pricing since local RRP always runs above the US headline figure once GST and import costs are factored in.

What we'll update

We'll keep an eye on NZ carrier and retailer pricing for the 17e as it settles post-launch, and will fold in any confirmed detail about the next "e" model once Apple's cycle moves that direction again.
