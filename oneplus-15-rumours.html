OnePlus 15 Rumours: Everything We Know So Far

Unlike most phones on our rumour list, the OnePlus 15 isn't hypothetical anymore, it launched in China in late 2025 and went global soon after. Here's what's actually confirmed hardware versus what's still shaking out for local NZ availability and the cheaper 15R.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Why we're labelling this so carefully: Twisti's whole value is showing you real, current prices — not guesses. Most of what's below is now confirmed hardware, but NZ-specific pricing and the 15R rollout still involve some uncertainty, so we've kept the same honest labelling system.

What's confirmed

Confirmed

The OnePlus 15 debuted in China on October 27, 2025 and went global on November 13, 2025, officially powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 with 12GB or 16GB RAM and 256GB, 512GB or 1TB storage options.

Confirmed

The display is a confirmed 6.78-inch AMOLED panel at 1272x2772 resolution with a 165Hz refresh rate and up to 1800 nits brightness, paired with a 7300mAh battery supporting up to 120W fast charging.

What's reported (analyst / supply-chain sourced)

Reported

US pricing has been reported at $899 for the 12GB/256GB configuration and $999 for 16GB/512GB, according to PhoneArena and GSMArena, though NZ retail pricing may differ once local stock and GST are factored in.

Reported

The camera system is reported as a 50MP main, 50MP ultrawide and 50MP telephoto trio, with the telephoto reportedly expanding from 3x to 3.5x zoom and the 32MP selfie camera gaining autofocus for the first time on a OnePlus flagship.

Reported

Design-wise, outlets report OnePlus has dropped its signature circular camera island in favour of a rounded-corner rectangular bump tucked into the top-left corner of the back panel, a notable departure from recent generations.

Reported

The cheaper OnePlus 15R is reported to have started rolling out from January 15, 2026, with UK pricing tipped from £649, though Twisti hasn't yet confirmed a firm NZ retail listing or launch date for the 15R locally.

Twisti's opinion

Opinion

Because the OnePlus 15 is already shipping, this is one of the safer phones on our rumour list to actually buy rather than wait on, assuming you can find confirmed NZ retail stock. If you're specifically chasing the lower price point, keep an eye on Twisti for the 15R's local arrival rather than importing, since warranty support tends to be smoother when buying from an NZ-registered retailer.

What we'll update

We'll update this page with confirmed NZ retail pricing and the 15R's local launch date as soon as local stores list stock, and we'll move any remaining specs from reported to confirmed once verified against local retailer listings.
