Garmin Fenix 8 NZ Price Guide

The Garmin Fenix 8 is the current flagship in Garmin's rugged multisport line, and NZ pricing varies a lot depending on size and screen type.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
How we picked these: Pricing gathered from current NZ retailer stock at Rebel Sport, Noel Leeming, and Garmin's own NZ store, cross-checked against manufacturer specification sheets.

What's in the Fenix 8 lineup

The Garmin Fenix 8 is Garmin's current-generation rugged flagship watch, sold in NZ in multiple case sizes (43mm, 47mm, and 51mm) and with either a standard AMOLED display or, on select models, a built-in flashlight. This is not a rumoured or upcoming device — it's the model currently shipping and sold across Garmin's NZ retail network.

Current NZ pricing by size and finish

  • Fenix 8, 43mm AMOLED: around $1,399 NZD.
  • Fenix 8, 47mm AMOLED: around $1,499 NZD.
  • Fenix 8, 51mm AMOLED with flashlight: around $1,799 NZD.
  • Fenix 8 Solar variants: a further premium of roughly $150–$250 NZD over equivalent AMOLED sizes.

These are sold through Rebel Sport, Noel Leeming, and Garmin's own NZ store, with occasional stock at Harvey Norman.

Key specs

The Fenix 8 includes multi-band GPS, a full-colour AMOLED touchscreen (with button-only navigation still available for glove use or wet conditions), up to 28 days of battery life in smartwatch mode, and dive-rated water resistance to 100m. The flashlight variant adds an actual LED torch built into the case edge, genuinely useful for pre-dawn runs or camping rather than a gimmick.

Fenix 8 vs Epix Pro

Garmin's Epix Pro range shares a very similar AMOLED display and case options but generally sits a little cheaper, without the flashlight option and with slightly different bezel materials. If the flashlight isn't a priority, the Epix Pro (from around $1,199 NZD) covers almost identical functionality for less.

Which size to choose

The 43mm suits smaller wrists and is the lightest option for daily wear, though battery life is shorter than the larger cases. The 47mm is the most popular middle ground for most buyers. The 51mm is aimed at multi-day adventure use where maximum battery life and the flashlight matter more than day-to-day comfort.

Who the Fenix 8 suits

  • Trail runners and hikers wanting genuinely rugged build quality and long battery life.
  • Divers, thanks to the 100m water rating and dive-mode support on select variants.
  • Anyone upgrading from an older Fenix (6 or 7 series) wanting the AMOLED screen upgrade.

Who should look elsewhere

If you don't need multi-day battery life or rugged casing, Garmin's cheaper Forerunner or Venu ranges (from around $549 NZD) cover most running and fitness tracking needs for considerably less.

Where to buy in NZ

Rebel Sport frequently has the best bundled pricing with a spare band included. Garmin's own NZ store guarantees full colour and size availability, while Noel Leeming is worth checking for interest-free payment plan options.
