Amazon Echo Next-Gen Rumours: Everything We Know So Far

Amazon's next generation of Echo hardware — built specifically around its Alexa+ AI assistant — has already been unveiled and is rolling out. Here's what's official versus what's still being reported about wider availability.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Why we're labelling this so carefully: Amazon has confirmed the new Echo lineup exists and detailed its hardware, but exact New Zealand availability and pricing for every model hasn't been clearly confirmed by Amazon locally, so we're careful to separate global announcements from what's actually confirmed for NZ.

What's confirmed

Confirmed

Amazon officially introduced four new Echo devices built for Alexa+: Echo Dot Max, Echo Studio, Echo Show 8, and Echo Show 11, describing them as the most advanced Echo devices it has built.

Confirmed

The new lineup runs on two custom Amazon-designed chips, AZ3 and AZ3 Pro, both including an AI Accelerator for running on-device AI models. AZ3 powers an improved microphone array Amazon says improves wake-word detection by more than 50%; the Echo Studio, Show 8 and Show 11 use the more capable AZ3 Pro.

Confirmed

The new Echo Show models add a 13-megapixel camera and a sensor platform Amazon calls Omnisense, combining camera, audio, ultrasound, Wi-Fi radar and accelerometer data for ambient AI features. Echo Studio was redesigned and shrunk by 40% compared to the previous model while adding a high-excursion woofer and three full-range drivers.

What's reported

Reported

Tech coverage from outlets like Android Authority and TechRadar has focused on the US rollout timeline and Alexa+ subscription tie-in, with less clarity reported so far on international rollout timing, including for New Zealand and Australia specifically.

Reported

Coverage has generally framed this as Amazon's biggest Echo hardware overhaul in years, positioning the new silicon as a direct response to competition from Google's Gemini-powered smart speakers and Apple's rumoured HomePod refresh, though that competitive framing is analysis rather than anything Amazon has stated directly.

Twisti's opinion

Opinion

The hardware itself is real and impressive on paper — dedicated AI silicon in a smart speaker is a genuine step change from the previous generation. The open question for New Zealand shoppers is really about local Alexa+ feature parity and pricing once (or if) these models land through NZ retailers, since Amazon's international device rollouts have historically lagged the US by months. We'd treat any NZ pricing you see quoted right now as provisional until it appears on local retailer listings.

What we'll update

We'll update this page once NZ retailers list confirmed pricing and stock dates for the Echo Dot Max, Echo Studio, Echo Show 8 or Echo Show 11.
