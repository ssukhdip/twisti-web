LG in NZ: TVs, appliances and audio explained

LG covers more product categories than almost any other electronics brand sold in NZ. Here's how the naming and tiers break down so you're not guessing at a showroom.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026

A brand that spans everything

Unlike brands that specialise in one category, LG sells TVs, soundbars, fridges, washing machines, dryers, air conditioners, and monitors, all under one roof. That breadth is useful if you're furnishing a whole kitchen or living room and want matching aesthetics or connected features, but it also means LG's model naming conventions vary a lot by category and can be confusing to compare at a glance.

TVs: OLED, QNED and the everyday ranges

LG is one of the best-known names in OLED television technology, and its OLED TV range typically sits at the premium end, prized for strong contrast and per-pixel black levels. Below that sits LG's QNED range (LED-based with quantum dot and mini-LED technology in higher tiers), aimed at buyers who want strong picture quality without OLED pricing. Entry-level LED/UHD models fill out the budget end. Model numbers usually encode screen size, tier and year, so it's worth learning to read them rather than relying on the marketing name alone.

OLED vs QNED — the real trade-off

OLED TVs generally offer better contrast and viewing angles, particularly in darker rooms, and are usually the pick for movie and gaming enthusiasts who care about black levels. QNED models can get brighter, which suits well-lit living rooms, and typically cost less for a comparable screen size. If your room has a lot of natural light, QNED's brightness advantage may matter more day-to-day than OLED's contrast edge.

Appliances: fridges, washers and more

LG's whiteware range spans budget to premium tiers much like its TVs, with higher-tier models adding features like larger capacities, more wash/dry programmes, and smart connectivity through LG's ThinQ app. As with TVs, mid-tier models tend to offer the best balance of features to price — top-tier smart features are convenient but not essential for most households.

Where to buy and how to compare

LG products are widely stocked across Noel Leeming, Harvey Norman, JB Hi-Fi (mainly TVs and audio) and appliance-focused retailers. Because LG's catalogue is so broad, prices for the exact same model can vary more between retailers than for narrower product categories — comparing before buying tends to pay off more here than with single-category brands.

Model year matters: LG typically refreshes its TV and major appliance ranges annually. A similarly named model from a previous year can be significantly cheaper and still perform well — check the model number's year code before assuming you need the newest release.
