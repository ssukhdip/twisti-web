Samsung Galaxy XR 2 Rumours: Everything We Know So Far

The original Samsung Galaxy XR only launched in October 2025. Nobody at Samsung, Google or Qualcomm has said a word about a sequel — so this is less a rumour roundup and more an honest look at how early we actually are.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Why we're labelling this so carefully: There is genuinely very little to report here. We're separating what the first-generation device actually shipped with (which shapes what a sequel would plausibly improve) from speculation about timing, rather than inventing leaks that don't exist.

What's confirmed

Confirmed

Neither Samsung, Google nor Qualcomm has announced, teased, or confirmed a second-generation Galaxy XR headset. As of July 2026 there is no official roadmap, no leaked codename, and no stated timeline for a successor.

Confirmed

The original Galaxy XR launched at USD $1,799, built jointly by Samsung, Google and Qualcomm, running the Snapdragon XR2+ Gen 2 chipset with a 3,552×3,840 per-eye micro-OLED display and up to 90Hz refresh, as the first commercially available Android XR headset.

What's reported

Reported

Samsung has publicly described Galaxy XR as the first step in a broader XR roadmap that includes AI smart glasses, but coverage of that roadmap (from outlets like Forbes and Road to VR) has focused on the glasses form factor and software partnerships with Google, not a next headset generation specifically.

Reported

Industry commentary generally expects Samsung to follow a roughly 18-24 month cycle for major hardware revisions based on its Galaxy device history, but no outlet has reported a specific timeframe, spec change, or price for a Galaxy XR 2 as of this update.

Twisti's opinion

Opinion

We'd rather tell you there's nothing solid to report than invent a "leak." Mixed-reality headsets are still a slow-moving, low-volume category, and Samsung, Google and Qualcomm all have an interest in letting the Android XR platform mature with app developers before rushing a sequel. If you're in NZ and considering the original Galaxy XR, buy it based on what it does today rather than waiting on a successor that has no confirmed timeline.

What we'll update

We'll update this page the moment any credible supply-chain reporting (Kuo, Gurman, ETNews, SamMobile, or similar) surfaces on a next-generation Galaxy XR. Until then, treat any "Galaxy XR 2 leak" headline you see elsewhere with real scepticism.
