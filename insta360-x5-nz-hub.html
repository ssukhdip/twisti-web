Insta360 X5 NZ: Price, Specs & Where to Buy

The Insta360 X5 is the latest flagship 360-degree action camera, letting you shoot first and choose the angle later. Here's what it costs in NZ and who it actually suits.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
How we picked these: Pricing checked against live PB Tech, Noel Leeming and Photogear NZ listings across the Standard, Starter and Essentials bundle tiers, cross-referenced with PriceMe NZ aggregated pricing.

What a 360 camera actually does

Unlike a normal action camera pointed in one direction, the Insta360 X5 has two lenses shooting a full 360-degree sphere simultaneously. The reframing happens afterward in the Insta360 app — you can pull out any angle, follow a subject automatically, or export a standard flat video without ever having had to aim the camera correctly while filming. That "shoot now, frame later" workflow is the entire selling point, and it's genuinely useful for action sports, cycling, and any situation where you can't predict the best angle in the moment.

Key specs

  • Sensor: Dual 1/1.28-inch sensors, one of the largest sensor sizes in a 360 action camera to date
  • Video: Up to 8K 360-degree capture, with AI-enhanced stitching between the two lenses
  • Waterproofing: Rated waterproof to 15m without a housing, deeper than most direct rivals
  • Stabilisation: FlowState stabilisation plus horizon lock, so footage stays level even if the camera itself is tumbling
  • Invisible selfie stick: Insta360's signature trick — a selfie stick effectively disappears from 360 footage during reframing, making it look like a drone shot

NZ bundle pricing

The X5 is sold in NZ across a few bundle tiers. The Standard Bundle (camera plus basics) is the entry point, with NZ pricing seen from roughly $892-$967 depending on retailer, including a PB Tech listing at the Auckland Airport duty-free store around $967. Step-up bundles — the Starter Bundle and Essentials Bundle — add accessories like extra batteries, a case, lens guards and mounting hardware, pushing pricing up to roughly $1,100-$1,240 depending on what's included. Noel Leeming also lists the Standard version directly. Given how much bundle contents vary between retailers, compare what's actually included, not just the headline price.

Where to buy in NZ

PB Tech and Noel Leeming both stock the X5 in Standard configuration, and Ferntech — better known for DJI — also carries Insta360 gear as an authorised NZ reseller. Photogear NZ, a specialist camera retailer, is worth checking for bundle deals that include extra accessories at a lower combined price than buying separately.

Insta360 X5 vs GoPro Max vs DJI Osmo Pocket 3

GoPro's Max is the closest direct rival in the 360 category, though Insta360 has generally led on sensor size, stitching quality and app polish in recent generations. The more relevant comparison for a lot of buyers is actually the DJI Osmo Pocket 3, which shoots a fixed, forward-facing 1-inch sensor image instead of a full sphere — better image quality for straightforward vlogging, but none of the reframe-later flexibility. If you know your shot in advance, Osmo Pocket 3 wins on quality; if you don't (action sports, unpredictable moments, POV content), the X5's 360 capture is the safer bet.

Who should buy the X5

  • Action sports, cycling, POV content where you can't predict the best angle: the X5 is close to ideal.
  • Straightforward vlogging with a known, fixed angle: the DJI Osmo Pocket 3 will generally look better for the same money.
  • Want the invisible-selfie-stick drone-style shot without owning a drone: the X5's signature trick is genuinely worth the price of entry.
