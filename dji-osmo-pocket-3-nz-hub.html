DJI Osmo Pocket 3 NZ: Price, Specs & Where to Buy

The Osmo Pocket 3 is DJI's palm-sized 3-axis gimbal camera, built for stabilised handheld video without carrying a full camera rig. Here's what it costs in NZ and who it actually suits.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
How we picked these: Pricing checked directly against live listings at PB Tech, Noel Leeming, Ferntech and Photogear NZ, with PriceSpy/PriceMe used to confirm the low end of the market.

What the Osmo Pocket 3 actually is

The Osmo Pocket 3 is a fixed-lens camera built into a mechanical 3-axis gimbal, small enough to fit in a jacket pocket. It's aimed squarely at vloggers, content creators and anyone who wants smooth, stabilised handheld 4K footage without lugging a mirrorless camera and separate gimbal. The headline upgrade over the original Osmo Pocket line is a 1-inch sensor — a genuinely bigger jump in low-light and dynamic range performance than the spec sheet numbers alone suggest — plus a 2-inch rotating touchscreen that flips for vertical (portrait) shooting, which matters a lot for anyone posting to Instagram Reels or TikTok.

Specs that matter

  • Sensor: 1-inch CMOS, f/2.0 aperture
  • Video: Up to 4K/120fps, with a dedicated D-Log M colour profile for grading
  • Screen: 2-inch rotating touchscreen (portrait/landscape)
  • Stabilisation: Mechanical 3-axis gimbal plus DJI's ActiveTrack subject tracking
  • Battery: Roughly 166 minutes of 1080p/30fps recording per charge, DJI-rated
  • Weight: About 179g, genuinely pocketable

NZ pricing: Standard vs Creator Combo

The Osmo Pocket 3 is sold in NZ as two main configurations. The Standard bundle (camera, mini tripod stand and basic accessories) sits around the mid-$600s to low-$700s NZD, with aggregated NZ pricing showing a low of roughly $675 across compared retailers. The Creator Combo adds a wide-angle lens, an extra battery handle with a bigger 950mAh cell, a proper mini tripod, a DJI Mic 2 wireless transmitter and a windscreen — genuinely useful additions for anyone shooting interviews or outdoor vlogs — and runs roughly $900-$1,150 NZD depending on retailer and sales, since it effectively bundles in a wireless mic system that would cost $150-200 on its own.

Where to buy it in NZ

PB Tech and Noel Leeming both stock the Osmo Pocket 3 in Standard and Creator Combo configurations. Ferntech, as DJI's officially authorised NZ retailer and repair centre, is worth considering if warranty support matters to you — DJI hardware, being mechanical (moving gimbal parts), has more failure points than a phone or laptop. Specialist camera retailers like Photogear NZ and Drone Depot also carry it and occasionally beat the big-box stores on bundle pricing.

Who it's actually for

If you already shoot on a phone and just want noticeably smoother, better-looking handheld video without learning a full camera system, the Osmo Pocket 3 is close to ideal — it's simpler to operate than a mirrorless camera with a gimbal, and the footage quality punches well above its size. It's less compelling if you mostly shoot static content, need interchangeable lenses, or are already happy with your phone's built-in stabilisation for casual use. Compare it against the Insta360 X5 if you specifically want 360-degree reframing flexibility instead of a fixed forward-facing lens.

Buying advice

  • Casual vlogger, tight budget: Standard bundle is enough.
  • Interviews, outdoor content, want wireless audio sorted too: Creator Combo is worth the premium since it includes a DJI Mic 2.
  • Want maximum framing flexibility, don't mind reframing in post: consider the Insta360 X5 instead.
