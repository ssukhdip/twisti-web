Galaxy Z Flip 8 Rumours: Everything We Know So Far

Unlike most phones on this list, the Z Flip 8 is rumoured to be weeks away rather than months, which means the leaks are unusually specific. Here's the breakdown of what's locked in versus what's still shifting.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Why we're labelling this so carefully: Twisti's whole value is showing you real, current prices — not guesses. None of the pricing below is confirmed. We're publishing it because people are genuinely searching for this information already, but we'd rather be upfront that it's industry speculation than let a rumour read like a fact.

What's confirmed

Confirmed

Samsung has not sent official Unpacked invitations for the Z Flip 8 at the time of writing. Everything here, including the widely reported July 22 date, still comes from leakers and outlets rather than a Samsung press release.

What's reported (analyst / supply-chain sourced)

Reported

Multiple outlets, including SamMobile and Gadget Hacks, report Samsung will hold Unpacked on July 22, 2026, unveiling the Z Flip 8 alongside the Z Fold 8 and a new Z Fold 8 Wide, with general retail availability tipped for August.

Reported

Display sizes are rumoured to carry over from the Z Flip 7: a 6.9-inch inner screen and 4.1-inch cover display, both on Dynamic AMOLED 2X panels with 120Hz refresh and up to 2,600 nits peak brightness, according to Tech Times and PhoneArena.

Reported

Industry reports point to a new "crease-free design structure" for the hinge, which would make the fold line far less visible than on any previous Z Flip, alongside a slimmer body tipped at roughly 13.2mm folded and 6.6mm unfolded.

Reported

Chipset rumours split by region again: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for most markets, Exynos 2600 reportedly reserved for Europe and India, paired with 12GB RAM and either 256GB or 512GB storage. A 4,300mAh battery with 25W wired charging is also tipped.

Reported

PhoneArena reports a possible starting price of $1,200 USD, with a price hike tipped across the whole Z lineup this cycle. A separate Weibo leak, unverified, claims this could be the last Flip if Samsung shifts focus to tri-fold devices, though that rumour should be treated with heavy skepticism.

Twisti's opinion

Opinion

This is the rare case on our rumour list where waiting is genuinely sensible if a July 22 Unpacked date holds, since it's only a matter of weeks away. If the reported price hike lands, expect Z Flip 7 stock to get discounted hard by NZ retailers in the run-up to launch, which is worth watching even if you end up preferring the older model's lower price over the Flip 8's rumoured but unconfirmed hinge upgrade.

What we'll update

The moment Samsung confirms Unpacked, we'll lock in the release date and swap the pricing section for real NZ carrier and retailer figures as they land.
