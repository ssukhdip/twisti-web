iPhone 17 Pro NZ: price, specs and where to buy

A running reference for anyone shopping an iPhone 17 Pro in New Zealand — storage tiers, what actually changed from the 16 Pro, and how to avoid overpaying.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026

Storage tiers and who needs which

iPhone 17 Pro is sold in 256GB, 512GB and 1TB configurations in NZ, with 256GB now the base tier. For most people who shoot photos and video casually and lean on iCloud, 256GB is enough. Videographers shooting ProRes, or anyone who never deletes anything, should look at 512GB — the jump to 1TB is really only worth it if you're recording a lot of 4K ProRes footage locally without offloading regularly.

What actually changed from iPhone 16 Pro

  • Chip — a newer A-series chip with a meaningful GPU uplift, most noticeable in sustained gaming and video export speed.
  • Camera system — refined telephoto performance and improved low-light processing across all three rear lenses.
  • Battery — incremental gains from chip efficiency rather than a bigger cell, so don't expect a dramatic jump in screen-on time.
  • Build — same titanium-adjacent design language as recent Pro models, with only minor cosmetic changes.

Is it worth upgrading from a 15 Pro or 14 Pro?

From a 15 Pro, the upgrade is genuinely marginal unless you specifically want the camera and chip improvements — most people should wait another cycle. From a 14 Pro or older, the combined gains in camera quality, chip speed and battery efficiency make it a much easier case, especially if your current battery health has started to noticeably decline.

Where to actually buy it in NZ

iPhone 17 Pro is stocked at Apple's own NZ store, JB Hi-Fi, Noel Leeming and PB Tech, along with the telco carriers on payment plans. List price is generally identical across the big retailers since Apple sets recommended pricing tightly, so the real savings come from bundled trade-in offers, occasional retailer cashback promotions, or timing your purchase around a sale event rather than expecting a straight price difference between stores.

Careful with "unlocked" grey imports. Cheaper listings for iPhone 17 Pro from overseas or grey-market sellers often lack local warranty support and may be region-locked for eSIM or carrier features. Buying from an authorised NZ retailer costs more up front but protects your Apple warranty and Consumer Guarantees Act rights.

The bottom line

If you're due an upgrade and coming from an iPhone 14 Pro or earlier, iPhone 17 Pro is a solid, low-risk buy from any of the major NZ retailers — the differences between stores come down to trade-in value and bundled extras, not the sticker price itself.
