iPhone 18 Pro Rumours: Camera, Design & Price Predictions

We've already covered the iPhone 18's broader launch timing elsewhere on Twisti. This post digs specifically into the camera and design changes leakers are describing for the Pro models, which sound like the biggest shift to the rear camera in years.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Why we're labelling this so carefully: Twisti's whole value is showing you real, current prices — not guesses. None of the pricing below is confirmed. We're publishing it because people are genuinely searching for this information already, but we'd rather be upfront that it's industry speculation than let a rumour read like a fact.

What's confirmed

Confirmed

Apple has not confirmed any camera or design specifics for the iPhone 18 Pro. As usual, Apple stays silent until its September event, so everything below is sourced from supply-chain reporting and leaked prototypes rather than Apple itself.

What's reported (analyst / supply-chain sourced)

Reported

MacRumors reports the variable aperture camera has entered production, with the main 48-megapixel sensor gaining a mechanically adjustable aperture that can shift between f/1.6 and f/4.0, replacing the fixed f/1.78 lens Apple has used across four generations.

Reported

Reports differ slightly on which model gets what: some outlets say the variable aperture lands on the Pro Max specifically as a first for any iPhone, while the standard Pro instead gets improved telephoto optics rather than the adjustable aperture.

Reported

A leaked prototype image, covered by Notebookcheck, appears to confirm rumours of a considerably smaller Dynamic Island, reportedly shrinking by around 35 percent from roughly 20.76mm to 13.49mm in width as Apple moves some Face ID sensors beneath the display.

Reported

MacRumors also reports this is expected to be the first of a four-part camera upgrade plan Apple is rolling out across future iPhone generations, suggesting variable aperture is just the opening move rather than a one-off feature.

Reported

No pricing specifics tied to the camera or design changes have leaked yet. Given variable aperture requires new mechanical hardware inside the camera module, some analysts speculate it could support a price increase on the Pro Max specifically, but this is pure speculation with no sourcing behind a number.

Twisti's opinion

Opinion

Variable aperture is a genuinely interesting upgrade rather than a marketing spec, since it directly affects depth of field and low-light shooting in a way most annual camera bumps don't. If photography is the main reason you'd upgrade, this generation looks worth the wait once it lands in September. If you mainly care about everyday performance and a smaller notch isn't a dealbreaker, the iPhone 17 Pro remains a strong buy and NZ pricing on it should soften as September approaches.

What we'll update

We'll confirm the exact aperture range and which models get it once Apple's keynote happens, and we'll add real NZ pricing the day Apple's local store goes live rather than estimating from US figures.
