Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Twisti ("we", "us", or "our") operates twisti.org. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

  • Email address and name when you create an account
  • Google profile information if you sign in with Google (name, email, profile photo)
  • Products you search for and save
  • Price alerts you set up

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To send price drop alerts for products you're watching
  • To send our weekly NZ deals newsletter (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • To improve search results and product recommendations

Google Sign In

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google. We do not receive your Google password or access to your Google account data beyond basic profile information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties except as required to operate our service (e.g. hosting providers).

When you click "Visit store" links, you are directed to third-party retailer websites. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave Twisti.

Cookies

We use localStorage to keep you signed in. We use Vercel Analytics to count page visits — this does not identify individual users.

Your Rights

  • Request a copy of your data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Unsubscribe from emails at any time

To exercise these rights, email us at hello@twisti.org

Contact

Questions? Email hello@twisti.org
