Smiths City NZ: Price Match, Returns & What to Know

Smiths City sells a wide mix of appliances, furniture and electronics, often alongside finance deals. Here's what to check before you commit.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026

What Smiths City actually sells

Smiths City is best known for furniture and whiteware, but its electronics range — TVs, audio gear, small appliances and some computing accessories — is significant enough that it regularly shows up in price comparisons alongside dedicated electronics retailers. Because the store spans so many categories, pricing strategy can vary noticeably between departments, so it pays to treat each purchase on its own merits rather than assuming a consistent discount pattern store-wide.

Price match: what to confirm

Retailers like Smiths City have periodically offered some form of price beat or match guarantee against competitors' advertised prices, but the specific terms — which competitors qualify, whether online-only retailers count, and what stock conditions apply — shift over time. Always confirm the current policy directly with staff or on their website rather than relying on older information.

Before requesting a price match

Screenshot or print the competing price with date and stock status visible, check the model number matches exactly, and ask whether the match applies to the ticketed price or an already-discounted sale price, since these are often treated differently.

Finance and interest-free offers

Smiths City has a long history of promoting interest-free finance periods on larger purchases like TVs and whiteware. These can be genuinely useful for spreading cost, but the cash price is often the better deal if you can pay upfront — finance offers sometimes come with establishment fees or higher underlying prices that offset the "interest-free" framing. Read the finance contract terms carefully, including what happens if a payment is missed.

  • Ask for the cash price separately from the finance price to compare directly.
  • Check for establishment or account fees that aren't part of the advertised rate.
  • Confirm the exact interest-free period length in writing.

Returns and warranty

As with any NZ retailer, the Consumer Guarantees Act sets a baseline for faulty goods regardless of store policy. Smiths City's own change-of-mind return conditions — timeframes, receipt requirements, restocking fees on larger items — are worth confirming at purchase, especially for bulky electronics like TVs where return logistics can be more involved.

Good to know: Delivery timeframes for larger electronics and appliances can vary by region, so if timing matters, ask for a specific delivery window before paying rather than assuming next-day availability.

Bottom line

Smiths City can be competitive on TVs, audio and appliances, particularly during promotional periods, but it's worth comparing the actual price against dedicated electronics retailers rather than assuming a furniture-and-whiteware store is automatically cheaper or pricier on tech.
