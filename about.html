Our Story

Built by a family who felt the pinch

🌿

Twisti was born from a simple frustration: why should finding the best price take hours of tab-switching, forum-scrolling, and guesswork? As a family who cares deeply about making every dollar count, we wanted something better — not just for us, but for every New Zealander dealing with the rising cost of living.

We're not a faceless corporation. Twisti is a family-driven project built with the belief that technology should give something back to the community. We built it ourselves, and we keep it free, because helping others stretch their budget further is the whole point.

What We Do

New Zealand's first AI-powered price comparison

Twisti scans prices across all major NZ retailers in real time — JB Hi-Fi, Harvey Norman, PB Tech, Noel Leeming, The Warehouse, Farmers, and more — and surfaces them in one clean view. No ads pushing you toward a sponsor. No hidden ranking. Just honest prices, ranked cheapest first.

But we went further. Twisti's AI verdict doesn't just show you prices — it tells you whether to buy right now. It weighs up the price spread, historical trends, product pros and cons, and even suggests better alternatives if something cheaper exists. It's like having a knowledgeable friend who's already done the research.

Our Vision

The future of shopping is already here

We're at the beginning of a shift in how people buy things. Right now, finding a great deal still takes effort — you have to know where to look, compare across sites, and hope you're not missing something cheaper elsewhere.

We believe that future looks very different: AI that knows what you need, finds the best deal automatically, and tells you exactly when to act — with minimal effort from you. Not hours of hunting. Just the answer, on your phone, when you need it.

Twisti is our step toward that future. We're expanding our AI capabilities, growing our store coverage, and building tools that turn deal-hunting from a chore into something that simply happens in the background. The best deal, on your fingertips, before you even had to ask.
