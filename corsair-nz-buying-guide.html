Corsair in NZ: The Complete Buying Guide

Corsair is as much a PC components brand as a peripherals brand in NZ, spanning everything from $65 mice to power supplies and RGB memory. Here's the range broken down with real local pricing.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
How we picked these: We compared current PB Tech listings across Corsair's keyboard, mouse, headset and power supply categories, since PB Tech carries the widest Corsair range of any NZ retailer and publishes granular per-SKU pricing.

Where to buy Corsair in NZ

PB Tech runs a dedicated Corsair storefront covering gaming keyboards, mice, headsets, power supplies, cases, cooling and memory -- it's comfortably the most complete Corsair range available from a single NZ retailer. Computer Lounge NZ also carries a solid Corsair selection focused on the gaming gear and components side. Because Corsair straddles both "gaming peripherals" and "PC components" categories, it's worth checking a components-focused retailer like PB Tech rather than a general electronics store like JB Hi-Fi or Noel Leeming, which tend to stock a much narrower slice of the brand (mostly headsets and pre-built mice, not power supplies or memory).

Keyboards -- broad range, real budget options

Corsair's gaming keyboard range at PB Tech starts from around $85-98 NZD for entry-level mechanical models and climbs through the K-series (K70, K100) toward premium options with per-key RGB and hot-swappable switches. This is a genuinely wide spread -- Corsair doesn't only compete at the premium end, and the entry-level K-series models are priced to compete directly with budget mechanical keyboards from Redragon and similar value brands, while still carrying Corsair's build quality and iCUE software ecosystem.

Mice -- from $65 to $100 wireless

Corsair gaming mice at PB Tech start from around $65 NZD for wired entry models, with wireless options around $87-100 NZD for mid-range models like the Harpoon or Katar wireless lines. This positions Corsair as one of the more budget-accessible gaming mouse brands in NZ relative to Razer and Logitech's equivalent wireless tiers, without stepping down to generic no-name mice.

Headsets -- accessory-focused range in NZ

PB Tech's Corsair headset listings lean toward headset accessories and mid-range gaming headsets around the $86-100 NZD mark, rather than Corsair's higher-end Virtuoso wireless range, which has less consistent NZ stock availability. If you specifically want the flagship Virtuoso RGB Wireless, it's worth checking stock directly at PB Tech or considering import options, since it's not always sitting in NZ warehouse stock.

Power supplies -- where Corsair is a genuinely default recommendation

Corsair PSUs are one of the most consistently recommended power supply brands among NZ PC building communities, and PB Tech's range starts from around $174-200 NZD for solid mid-range units, climbing to around $214-246 NZD for 850W fully-modular models suited to a mid-to-high-end gaming PC build. For anyone building or upgrading a desktop PC in NZ, Corsair sits alongside Seasonic as one of the two most trusted PSU brands stocked locally, and the RM and CX series are common default picks in NZ PC build guides.

Bottom line

Corsair is worth shopping specifically at a components-focused retailer like PB Tech rather than a general electronics store, since that's where the full range -- not just headsets and mice -- is actually available. It's a strong value pick across keyboards and mice, and close to a default recommendation for power supplies in any NZ PC build.
