Canon EOS R6 Mark III Rumours: What to Expect

The rumour phase for the R6 Mark III is technically over, Canon officially announced it back in November 2025. We're keeping this page up as a straight, honest reference for anyone still comparing what leaked against what actually shipped.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Why we're labelling this so carefully: Twisti's whole value is showing you real, current prices — not guesses. The R6 Mark III is now a fully official, shipping product, so most of this page is confirmed. We've kept our usual labelling for consistency and for the NZ pricing detail that can still shift by retailer.

What's confirmed

Confirmed

Canon officially unveiled the EOS R6 Mark III on November 6, 2025, alongside the RF 45mm f/1.2 STM lens. It features a 32.5-megapixel full-frame sensor with electronic shutter shooting up to 40fps and 20 frames of pre-continuous capture, or mechanical/electronic first curtain shutter up to 12fps.

Confirmed

US pricing was confirmed at $2,799 for the body only, with kit bundles including the RF24-105 F4 L IS USM lens at $4,049, the RF24-105 F4-7.1 IS STM USM lens at $3,149, and a Stop Motion Animation Firmware bundle at $2,899.

Confirmed

Canon confirmed the R6 Mark III does not include a top LCD display, a feature Canon is reserving for its more expensive R-series bodies, keeping the R6 III positioned as a hybrid enthusiast and content-creator camera rather than a flagship pro body.

What's reported (analyst / supply-chain sourced)

Reported

Twisti has not yet located a firm, widely confirmed NZ retail price for the R6 Mark III. Canon's NZ pricing on recent R-series bodies has typically landed above a straight USD conversion once GST and distributor costs are added, so expect a similar pattern here.

Reported

Canon Watch describes the update as "incremental innovation," suggesting reviewers see this as a refinement of the R6 II's formula rather than a dramatic leap, which is worth knowing if you're deciding whether to upgrade from the previous generation.

Twisti's opinion

Opinion

Since the R6 Mark III is already shipping, this is a straightforward buy decision rather than a waiting game. If you already own an R6 II, the improvements here look evolutionary rather than essential, and it may be worth letting R6 II prices drop on Twisti instead. If you're coming from an older body or a different system entirely, the confirmed 40fps burst and pre-continuous shooting make a genuine case for the newer model.

What we'll update

We'll add confirmed NZ retail pricing as soon as we verify it against multiple authorised Canon dealers, and note any firmware updates that materially change the camera's capabilities.
