Google Pixel 11 Rumours: Price, Specs & Release Window

Google keeps its Pixel launches on a tight annual schedule, so the Pixel 11 rumour mill has plenty to work with already. Here's the split between what's genuinely locked in and what's still a leak.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Why we're labelling this so carefully: Twisti's whole value is showing you real, current prices — not guesses. None of the pricing below is confirmed. We're publishing it because people are genuinely searching for this information already, but we'd rather be upfront that it's industry speculation than let a rumour read like a fact.

What's confirmed

Confirmed

Google hasn't issued a formal Pixel 11 announcement yet, but it has confirmed a Made by Google event pattern that lands every August, so a launch this coming August is effectively locked in by precedent even though Google hasn't sent invites.

What's reported (analyst / supply-chain sourced)

Reported

Droid Life and Android Central both report the Made by Google event is expected around August 12 in New York, following the same late-summer slot Google has used for recent Pixel launches.

Reported

The Tensor G6 chip is reportedly being built on TSMC's 2nm process, a first for Pixel after years on Samsung's foundry, and will apparently be paired with a MediaTek M90 modem, ending Tensor's long-running reliance on Samsung's Exynos modems.

Reported

Camera leaks point to a broad overhaul: a new 50MP main sensor across the standard Pixel 11 and the Pro Fold, while the Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL are tipped to get all-new main and telephoto lenses, according to The Gadgeteer and PhoneArena.

Reported

A cosmetic feature called "Pixel Glow" is rumoured, adding RGB LED notification lighting inside the camera bar in place of the temperature sensor Google added on recent Pro models.

Reported

Forbes reports pricing may hold at $899 for the base Pixel 11, now starting at 256GB storage since the 128GB tier is expected to be dropped. The Pixel 11 Pro is rumoured to avoid any increase, starting around $1,099, while leaks suggest the Pro XL could see a $100 bump across every storage tier.

Twisti's opinion

Opinion

An August launch means this one is close enough that waiting could genuinely make sense if you're due an upgrade, especially since NZ pricing on Pixel phones has historically tracked US pricing fairly closely once converted. If you need a phone in the next month or two, though, the Pixel 10 range is a known quantity with a proven camera stack, and Twisti regularly sees its prices soften right before a new Pixel lands as retailers clear stock.

What we'll update

Once Google sends event invites we'll update the release date to confirmed status, and we'll swap in real NZ pricing the day local retailers list it rather than converting US figures ourselves.
