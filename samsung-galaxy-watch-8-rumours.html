Samsung Galaxy Watch 8 Rumours: Everything We Know So Far

Update: the Galaxy Watch 8 stopped being a rumour a year ago. Samsung announced it on 9 July 2025 and it's been on New Zealand shelves since late July 2025. This page now covers the confirmed specs and pricing, plus how the pre-launch leaks compared to what actually shipped.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Why we're labelling this so carefully: Because this started as a pre-launch rumour tracker, we're keeping it structured around confirmed facts versus what was reported beforehand, so returning readers can see exactly how the rumour mill performed rather than a rewritten "clean" review.

What's confirmed

Confirmed

Samsung announced the Galaxy Watch 8 series at Galaxy Unpacked in Brooklyn on 9 July 2025, with Watch 8, Watch 8 Classic and Watch Ultra all launching together and going on sale 25 July 2025. It was Samsung's first smartwatch line to ship with Wear OS 6.

Confirmed

Both Galaxy Watch 8 models run Samsung's Exynos W1000 chip on a 3nm process. The case is 11% slimmer than the Watch 7 at 8.6mm, and the display peaks at 3000 nits — 50% brighter than the previous generation. The 40mm model has a 325mAh battery, the 44mm has 435mAh, and the Watch 8 Classic has 445mAh.

Confirmed

US pricing launched at $349.99 for the 40mm Watch 8 (a $50 increase over the Watch 7), $379 for the larger display option, and an extra $50 for LTE. The Watch 8 Classic started at $499. In New Zealand, the Watch 8 has typically sold from around $423-$450 for Bluetooth-only 40mm/44mm models, rising to roughly $520-$630 for LTE variants, depending on retailer.

What's reported

Reported

Pre-launch coverage from outlets like SamMobile and Android Central correctly anticipated the squircle case design carrying over from the Galaxy Watch Ultra's cushion shape, and correctly forecast a slimmer profile ahead of Samsung's official reveal.

Reported

Some pre-launch rumours speculated about a significantly larger battery jump than what actually shipped; the real-world capacity increases were more modest than several leak roundups had suggested.

Twisti's opinion

Opinion

A year on, the Galaxy Watch 8 remains a solid buy in NZ if you're inside the Samsung/Android ecosystem — the brighter, slimmer display and Wear OS 6 software still feel current, and prices have settled well below launch RRP at most retailers. If a Galaxy Watch 9 is announced at the next Unpacked event (historically around July/August), that's the point we'd suggest holding out for a newer model rather than paying near-launch pricing for the Watch 8 today.

What we'll update

We'll update this page with any confirmed details on a Galaxy Watch 9 as soon as Samsung's next Unpacked event is scheduled.
