3. How We Collect Information

We collect information directly from you when you register, sign in or use the Service. We also collect limited anonymous data automatically (session ID, page visits). We do not purchase personal information from third-party data brokers.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Sending price drop notifications for products you are tracking
  • Sending confirmation emails when you set a price alert
  • Personalising AI Verdicts based on your search history and budget preferences
  • Improving the accuracy and relevance of search results
  • Responding to your enquiries or support requests
  • Sending our weekly deals digest, if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Complying with legal obligations

We do not use your personal information for automated profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects without your knowledge.

6. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Vercel (hosting, analytics), Neon (database, AWS Sydney), Upstash (caching), Anthropic (AI Verdict generation). These providers process data on our behalf under appropriate data processing obligations.
  • Google OAuth: When you sign in with Google, authentication is handled by Google. We receive only your basic profile. Google's privacy policy applies to that interaction.
  • Affiliate links: When you click "Visit store", you are directed to a third-party Retailer. We may earn a commission on resulting purchases. We do not share your personal information with Retailers.
  • Email delivery: Confirmation and alert emails are sent via Namecheap Private Email (hello@twisti.org). Your email address is used solely to send you emails you have requested.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Twisti, our users or the public.

7. Affiliate Links and Commissions

Twisti is free to use. We participate in affiliate programmes including Commission Factory. If you purchase through a link on Twisti, we may receive a commission from the Retailer. You pay nothing extra. The cheapest price is always shown first regardless of affiliate relationships.

8. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on servers located in Sydney, Australia (Neon PostgreSQL on AWS ap-southeast-2). We use HTTPS/TLS encryption, bcrypt password hashing, JWT authentication, and rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure — if you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at hello@twisti.org.

9. Retention of Personal Information

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where required by law to retain certain records.

10. Your Rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and Australian Privacy Act 1988, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information held by us
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing emails (unsubscribe link in every email)
  • Make a complaint if you believe we have breached your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@twisti.org. We will respond within 20 working days as required under the NZ Privacy Act 2020.

If you are in New Zealand and not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

If you are in Australia, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

11. Children's Privacy

Twisti is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Third-Party Websites

Twisti contains links to third-party Retailer websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before making a purchase.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date and version number. If changes are material, we will notify registered users by email. Continued use of Twisti after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
