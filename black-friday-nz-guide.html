Black Friday NZ: what actually goes on sale

Which categories genuinely see their best pricing of the year on Black Friday, and how to tell a real discount from an inflated "was" price.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026

What genuinely gets its best pricing on Black Friday

  • TVs — this is consistently one of the strongest categories, especially on outgoing model-year TVs being cleared before the new range lands early the following year.
  • Headphones and audio — flagship noise-cancelling headphones and speakers see genuine, meaningful cuts, often their deepest of the year outside a generation changeover.
  • Small appliances — air fryers, coffee machines and vacuums see real, comparison-worthy discounts across most retailers.
  • Laptops — decent but less dramatic than TVs and audio; the best laptop deals tend to be bundle-based (extra RAM, accessories) rather than a huge straight price cut.

What tends to be weaker than the hype suggests

Current-generation flagship phones (the newest iPhone or Galaxy) rarely see meaningful direct discounts on Black Friday — manufacturers protect that pricing closely. Console hardware itself also rarely drops in price; what changes is the bundle around it. If a "huge Black Friday deal" on a brand-new flagship phone or console looks too good, check whether it's actually a bundle or trade-in-dependent offer rather than a straight price cut.

How to actually verify a Black Friday deal is real

Check the item's price history from several weeks before Black Friday, not just the days immediately prior — some retailers quietly raise prices in the lead-up so the "sale" price looks bigger than it is. A genuine Black Friday deal should beat the price from a month or more earlier, not just the price from last week.

Boxing Day is a separate, often better window for some categories. Black Friday tends to lead on electronics and audio; Boxing Day often has stronger appliance and TV clearance pricing as retailers push end-of-year stock. Worth comparing both rather than assuming Black Friday is automatically the year's best pricing across every category.

The bottom line

Black Friday is genuinely strong for TVs, audio and small appliances — treat phone and console "deals" with more scepticism, and always check price history before trusting the discount is real.
