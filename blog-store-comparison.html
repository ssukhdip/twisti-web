JB Hi-Fi vs Noel Leeming vs PB Tech: Which Actually Has the Best Prices?

Every NZ electronics shopper eventually asks this. The honest answer is: it depends what you're buying and what week it is — but here's how the three actually differ, so you know where to look first.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026

If you've shopped for electronics in New Zealand, you've bounced between these three tabs. All three sell a lot of the same gear, all three run sales constantly, and none of them is reliably cheapest across the board. Here's what actually sets them apart.

JB Hi-Fi

The most recognisable name for entertainment tech — TVs, audio, gaming, phones, and a genuinely large physical store footprint. JB Hi-Fi runs frequent sales and, anecdotally, tends to be one of the more flexible retailers when it comes to matching a competitor's advertised price in-store, though it's discretionary rather than a hard written guarantee for every case. Good starting point for phones, TVs, headphones, and gaming.

Noel Leeming

Part of The Warehouse Group, with the broadest range of the three once you include appliances — fridges, washing machines, and small appliances alongside the usual tech categories. Noel Leeming publishes a formal "Price Promise" policy. Worth knowing: in July 2025, the Commerce Commission filed proceedings against Noel Leeming alleging its Price Promise had conditions that made it hard for customers to actually claim in practice — Noel Leeming has disputed this. Worth reading the current terms carefully before assuming a match will be straightforward, and worth checking Twisti's price comparison directly rather than relying on the promise to catch a better deal elsewhere.

PB Tech

Started as a computer hardware specialist and still leans that way — PC components, peripherals, networking gear, and business/IT equipment tend to be genuinely competitive here, often cheaper than the two above for that category. It's not an online-only store either, with physical locations in several cities. PB Tech's own price-match policy (refund the difference if you find it cheaper elsewhere within 7 days) is one of the more customer-friendly on paper, though the usual "identical item, in stock, NZ retailer" conditions still apply.

So which one's actually cheapest?

None of them, consistently. Price leadership shifts by category and by week:

  • Phones and TVs — JB Hi-Fi and Noel Leeming trade the lead depending on current promotions.
  • Laptops, components, and anything "computer-y" — PB Tech is worth checking first.
  • Appliances — Noel Leeming's range advantage often means better selection, if not always the lowest price.

All three also run their own clearance and end-of-line sales on different schedules, so the "cheapest store" for a given product genuinely changes week to week.

The actual best strategy

Rather than picking a "favourite" store and hoping, check the specific product across all three before buying anything over about $100. Price match policies help, but only after you've already found the lower price somewhere — and most have enough conditions attached (identical model, in stock, NZ retailer only, sometimes excluding online-only listings) that they're a backup, not a first move.
