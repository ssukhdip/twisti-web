Garmin Fenix 9 Rumours: Everything We Know So Far

A stray line of code in an app update, a trademark filing nobody was meant to notice, and a CEO who's already talking about "the back half of the year" — that's roughly what the Garmin Fenix 9 rumour mill has to work with right now. Here's what's solid, what's shaky, and what's just people connecting dots.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Why we're labelling this so carefully: Twisti's whole value is showing you real, current prices — not guesses. None of the pricing below is confirmed. We're publishing it because people are genuinely searching for this information already, but we'd rather be upfront that it's industry speculation than let a rumour read like a fact.

What's confirmed

Confirmed

Garmin's current flagship rugged GPS watch line is the Fenix 8 series, including the Fenix 8 Pro. Any Fenix 9 would be the successor to that lineup, not a brand-new category — so expect the same broad multisport, outdoor-navigation positioning, just with newer internals.

What's reported (analyst / supply-chain sourced)

Reported

Launch window: August-September 2026 most likely

the5krunner reports that Garmin's own CEO has signalled a Fenix 9 launch in the second half of 2026, with the site pegging August-September as the most probable window based on Garmin's usual release cadence.

Reported

Hidden code reference in Garmin Connect app

TechRadar reports that a developer digging through the Garmin Connect Android app spotted a direct reference to "Garmin Fenix 9" in version 5.26 — a string that wasn't present in the prior 5.25 build. The same code dig turned up a reference to a Garmin Enduro 4, suggesting the two watches could be prepped for launch around the same time.

Reported

No standard microLED display

Despite years of microLED speculation around Garmin's flagship watches, the5krunner reports the technology failed to deliver the battery-life gains Garmin was hoping for when trialled in the Fenix 8 Pro, and won't appear as a standard Fenix 9 display. Expect AMOLED and Solar MIP configurations instead, with the possibility Garmin explores a solar-over-display hybrid if it finds a panel supplier that can make it work.

Reported

Faster chip, better sensors, near-certain

the5krunner lists a faster processor, refined health sensors, improved GPS accuracy, and better battery efficiency as near-certain upgrades — the kind of iterative improvements Garmin tends to deliver on every flagship refresh regardless of what else changes.

Reported

Tri-band GNSS: good odds, not guaranteed

the5krunner puts roughly an 80% chance on tri-band GNSS satellite positioning landing in the Fenix 9 this year, rising to 95% by 2027 if it's held back — reflecting how close Garmin appears to be on the feature without it being locked in yet.

Reported

"Muscle Battery" trademark and sizing leaks

Garmin has filed a trademark for "Muscle Battery," which outlets including Geeky Gadgets and Notebookcheck read as a possible hint at an SMO2-style muscle-oxygen sensor — though the filing alone doesn't confirm the sensor exists or which watch would get it. Separately, Smartwatch Insight and Sundried report the Fenix 9 is expected in three sizes (43mm, 47mm, 51mm) with premium material options like sapphire crystal and titanium carrying over from the Fenix 8 line.

Twisti's opinion

Opinion

Our read: the code reference is the strongest signal here

A CEO hinting at a second-half launch is the kind of thing companies say every year without committing to a date, and a trademark filing can sit unused for a long time. But finding "Garmin Fenix 9" as a literal string inside a shipped app build is a different order of evidence — someone on Garmin's own team put it there, even if by accident. Combined with the Enduro 4 reference in the same build, we'd bet on both watches landing together, most likely in that August-September window, before we'd bet on any single spec rumour panning out exactly as described.

What we'll update

We'll revise this page as soon as Garmin confirms a launch date, and again once official specs and NZ pricing land. If you're watching for a Fenix 8 price drop in the meantime, that's often the more useful signal that a new model is close.
