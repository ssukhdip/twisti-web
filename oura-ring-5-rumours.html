Oura Ring 5 Rumours: What to Expect

Most rumour roundups end with a launch that proves half the leaks wrong. Not this one. Oura's fifth-generation smart ring showed up on May 28, 2026 — more than a year earlier than industry chatter had predicted — and pre-orders opened the same afternoon. So this piece isn't really speculation anymore; it's a look at what actually shipped, plus the handful of things still unsettled for shoppers here in New Zealand.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Why we're labelling this so carefully: Twisti's whole value is showing you real, current prices — not guesses. None of the pricing below is confirmed. We're publishing it because people are genuinely searching for this information already, but we'd rather be upfront that it's industry speculation than let a rumour read like a fact.

What's confirmed

Confirmed

Oura Ring 5 is officially out — earlier than anyone expected

Oura announced the Ring 5 on May 28, 2026, with pre-orders live the same day and shipping beginning June 4, 2026. Earlier chatter had pegged a launch for late 2027, so this caught a lot of the wearables press off guard.

Confirmed

Preorder price is US$399

That's the headline preorder figure Oura published at launch. NZ retail pricing and any local conversion or shipping premium haven't been locked down publicly, which is where the "reported" section below comes in.

Confirmed

It's noticeably slimmer than the Ring 4

Thickness drops from 3.5mm to 2.8mm and width goes from 7.9mm down to 6mm — roughly a 40% reduction in bulk. Oura is calling it its thinnest ring to date.

Confirmed

New health tracking: GLP-1 insights, blood pressure, nighttime breathing

The Ring 5 adds proactive blood pressure information, nighttime breathing data, and insights aimed at people using GLP-1 medications — a clear signal Oura is chasing the broader metabolic-health market, not just sleep and recovery tracking.

Confirmed

Battery life holds at about a week

Despite the slimmer body, Oura says the Ring 5 still manages roughly a full week per charge — in line with what Ring 4 owners were already used to.

Confirmed

Three colours, two finishes

Deep Rose, black and silver are the launch colourways, each available in glossy or matte finish.

What's reported (analyst / supply-chain sourced)

Reported

NZ retail availability timing is still fuzzy

Coverage from outlets like Tom's Guide and TechAdvisor focused on the US pre-order and shipping window; neither has confirmed a firm on-shelf date for New Zealand retailers, so expect a lag behind the June US ship date rather than a same-week arrival.

Reported

Early demand is being read as a response to Samsung's Galaxy Ring push

Some of the launch-week commentary frames Oura's surprise early release as a competitive move against Samsung's Galaxy Ring gaining ground in the smart ring category — but that's analyst framing rather than anything Oura has stated outright.

Reported

Local pricing likely to land above a straight USD-to-NZD conversion

No NZ price has been published yet. Based on how Oura's previous rings were priced locally, expect the eventual NZD figure to sit above a simple currency conversion once GST and local distributor margins are factored in — but this is our own estimate, not a sourced figure.

Twisti's opinion

Opinion

The early launch is the real story here, not the spec sheet

The design and health-feature upgrades are welcome, but incremental. What's genuinely notable is Oura moving up its own roadmap by well over a year — a sign the smart ring category is heating up faster than expected and companies don't want to cede ground while they wait for a "perfect" release window. For NZ buyers, our advice is to hold off on paying import premiums and wait for confirmed local pricing before committing.

What we'll update

We'll refresh this page once NZ retailers confirm local pricing and stock dates, and again if Oura shares more detail on how the GLP-1 and blood pressure features are rolled out region by region.
