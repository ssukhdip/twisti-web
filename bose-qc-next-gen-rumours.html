Bose QuietComfort Next-Gen Rumours: What's Coming

Anyone searching for "next-gen Bose QuietComfort" right now is probably already looking at the 2nd Gen QuietComfort Ultra, since that's the newest model on shelves. Below is what's actually confirmed about it, plus what little is circulating about what Bose does next.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Why we're labelling this so carefully: Twisti's whole value is showing you real, current prices — not guesses. None of the pricing below is confirmed. We're publishing it because people are genuinely searching for this information already, but we'd rather be upfront that it's industry speculation than let a rumour read like a fact.

What's confirmed

Confirmed

Bose announced the QuietComfort Ultra Headphones (2nd Gen) on 10 September 2025, releasing 2 October 2025 at US$449. It added lossless USB-C audio, a refined adaptive noise-cancelling algorithm, and a new Cinema Mode for clearer dialogue on video content, with up to 30 hours of playback (23 hours with Immersive Audio on).

Confirmed

The matching QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen) arrived in black, white smoke or deep plum in summer 2025, with both the headphones and earbuds still Bose's current flagship lineup as of July 2026.

What's reported (analyst / supply-chain sourced)

Reported — Rolling Stone

Rolling Stone's product coverage of the 2nd Gen launch framed the earbuds and headphones as Bose consolidating around a single "Ultra" flagship tier rather than splitting focus across multiple simultaneous lines, which is worth knowing if you're trying to guess where the next update lands.

Reported — Techyorker

Tech outlets tracking Bose's roadmap describe likely future updates as incremental follow-ups focused on Bluetooth codec support, call quality and firmware, rather than a wholesale new headphone category — there is no confirmed 3rd Gen model or announcement date as of writing.

Reported — Headphone Infatuation

Coverage of Bose's 2026 lineup so far notes no new over-ear flagship has been announced this year, suggesting Bose is treating the 2nd Gen Ultra as a multi-year flagship rather than following an annual refresh cycle like some rivals.

Twisti's opinion

Opinion

Bose's slower refresh cadence compared with Sony and Samsung is actually good news for buyers — the 2nd Gen Ultra headphones are unlikely to be replaced any time soon, so NZ shoppers can buy with more confidence that they won't be outdated within months. If a 3rd Gen model does surface, expect it in 2027 at the earliest based on Bose's historical two-year gaps.

What we'll update

We'll update this page immediately if Bose announces a new QuietComfort model, and will keep tracking NZ pricing for the current 2nd Gen Ultra range in the meantime.
