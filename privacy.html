Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 June 2026 · Effective: 30 June 2026

This policy applies to users in New Zealand and Australia. It is written to comply with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and, where applicable, the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (including the Australian Privacy Principles).

Twisti ("we", "us", "our") operates twisti.org, a free price comparison service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly and transparently.

1. Who We Are

Twisti is a New Zealand-owned and operated service. Our primary contact for privacy matters is:

📧 hello@twisti.org

2. What Personal Information We Collect

We collect personal information only where it is reasonably necessary for our service. The table below outlines what we collect, why, and how.

InformationWhen collectedWhy we need it
Email address, nameWhen you create an accountTo identify your account and contact you about price alerts
Google profile (name, email, profile photo)If you sign in with GoogleTo authenticate you via Google OAuth. We do not access any other Google account data.
Search history (products searched)While you use TwistiTo personalise AI verdicts and improve recommendations over time
Saved products and price alertsWhen you save or set an alertTo notify you when a price drops
Session ID (anonymous)On every visitTo provide basic search personalisation without requiring an account
Page visit counts (Vercel Analytics)On every visitTo understand traffic patterns. This data is aggregated and does not identify individuals.

We do not collect sensitive information (as defined by the Australian Privacy Act 1988), including health information, financial credentials, government identifiers, or biometric data.

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information directly from you when you register, sign in, or use the service. We also collect limited anonymous information automatically (session ID, page visits). We do not buy or obtain personal information from third-party data brokers.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information only for purposes you would reasonably expect, including:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Sending price drop notifications for products you are watching
  • Personalising AI buying verdicts based on your search history
  • Improving the accuracy and relevance of search results
  • Responding to your enquiries or support requests
  • Sending our deals newsletter, if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Complying with legal obligations

We do not use your personal information for automated profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use third-party services to operate Twisti, including Vercel (hosting, analytics), Neon (database, based in AWS Sydney), Upstash (caching), and Anthropic (AI verdict generation). These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by appropriate data processing obligations.
  • Google OAuth: When you sign in with Google, authentication is handled by Google. We receive only your basic profile information. Google's privacy policy applies to that interaction.
  • Affiliate links: When you click "Visit store", you are directed to a third-party retailer. We may earn a small commission on resulting purchases. The retailer's own privacy policy applies once you leave Twisti. We do not share your personal information with retailers.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Twisti, our users, or the public.

6. Affiliate Links and Commissions

Twisti is free to use. To sustain the service, we participate in affiliate programmes (including Commission Factory). If you purchase a product through a link on Twisti, we may receive a small commission from the retailer at no cost to you. This never affects how prices are ranked — the cheapest price is always shown first, regardless of affiliate relationships.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your login token and theme preference on your device. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to any third party. Vercel Analytics uses a privacy-preserving page-view counter that does not set cookies and does not identify individual users.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

8. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on servers located in Sydney, Australia (Neon PostgreSQL on AWS ap-southeast-2). We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords (bcrypt), and access controls. No system is perfectly secure — if you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately at hello@twisti.org.

9. Retention of Personal Information

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain certain records.

10. Your Rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and Australian Privacy Act 1988, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information held by us
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing emails at any time (unsubscribe link in every email)
  • Make a complaint if you believe we have breached your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@twisti.org. We will respond within 20 working days as required under the NZ Privacy Act 2020.

If you are an Australian resident and are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au. If you are in New Zealand, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

11. Children's Privacy

Twisti is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Third-Party Websites

Twisti contains links to third-party retailer websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before making a purchase.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we will notify registered users by email. Continued use of Twisti after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

14. Contact and Complaints

For any privacy questions, access requests, or complaints, please contact us:

📧 hello@twisti.org
We aim to acknowledge all privacy requests within 5 working days and resolve them within 20 working days.
