Best Time to Buy Drones NZ

Drone prices in NZ move on two predictable cycles: the once-a-year Black Friday discount window, and the quieter price drops that follow every new DJI model launch.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
Two different discount mechanisms: Black Friday is a temporary sale event. A new DJI model launch is a permanent price shift that pushes the previous generation down for good. Knowing which one you're waiting for changes your timing strategy.

Black Friday: real but time-limited

NZ's authorised DJI retailers — Ferntech (DJI's official NZ store) and Drone Depot among them — run dedicated Black Friday sale pages each year with discounts across current-generation drones. As one concrete recent example, Drone Depot's Black Friday period had the DJI Mini 5 Pro Fly More Combo reduced from $2,108 to $1,929, a genuine but fairly modest saving (around $180, roughly 8-9%) on a current flagship model. Black Friday discounts on the newest DJI models tend to be moderate rather than dramatic, because DJI controls pricing tightly across its authorised retail network — you're very unlikely to see 30-40% off a drone that launched in the last six months.

The bigger price movement: DJI's release cycle

The more significant discount pattern comes from DJI's own product cycle. When DJI launches a new generation in a line (for example, a new Mini or Air model), the previous generation's price typically drops and stays down permanently, rather than through a short sale window, as retailers clear remaining stock and DJI itself repositions the older model. As of mid-2026, DJI's NZ lineup shows this pattern clearly: the DJI Mini 4 Pro (a now-previous-generation compact drone) is priced from around $1,319 body-only up to $2,399 for the Fly More Combo, sitting below newer models like the Mini 5 Pro and Air 3S, which starts around $2,788 for its Fly More Combo. Buying one generation behind DJI's current flagship is consistently the single biggest lever for drone value in NZ — often a genuinely capable drone at a meaningfully lower price than the newest release.

How to time a purchase practically

  • If you want the newest model regardless of price, Black Friday (late November) is still worth waiting for — the discount is real, just modest (commonly under 10%) on current-generation stock.
  • If you want the best value per dollar, buying the previous generation once a new model has launched typically beats waiting for a sale on the current one — the permanent post-launch price drop is usually larger than a seasonal sale discount.
  • Watch DJI's own announcement cadence — DJI has historically refreshed its Mini and Air lines roughly annually, so a model that's 10-12 months old is a reasonable bet for an upcoming price step-down.

Where to actually check current NZ pricing

Ferntech and DJI's own NZ store (djistore.co.nz) are DJI's authorised NZ retail channels and reliably reflect official current pricing; PriceSpy NZ and PriceMe are useful for comparing that official pricing against secondary retailers like Drone Depot at a glance before buying.
