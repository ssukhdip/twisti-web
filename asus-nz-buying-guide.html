ASUS in NZ: The Complete Buying Guide

ASUS covers everything from budget Chromebooks to $4,000+ ROG gaming rigs, and NZ pricing varies more between retailers than most people assume. Here's how to shop the brand properly.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
How we picked these: We compared current listings across PB Tech, JB Hi-Fi NZ and Noel Leeming, the three retailers that carry the broadest ASUS range in New Zealand, along with ASUS's own NZ site for RRP reference.

Where to actually buy ASUS in NZ

PB Tech carries the deepest ASUS catalogue in New Zealand by a wide margin -- laptops, monitors, routers, motherboards and components -- and is generally the best starting point for comparison shopping because of how often it runs line-item discounts on specific SKUs. JB Hi-Fi NZ and Noel Leeming both stock the consumer-facing ASUS laptop range (ZenBook, Vivobook, ROG, TUF) but with a narrower selection focused on what's likely to move off retail shelves, and they're worth checking specifically during their periodic storewide sales (Click Frenzy, Boxing Day, back-to-school in late January) since they'll sometimes beat PB Tech's everyday pricing during those windows.

ZenBook -- the premium ultrabook line

ASUS ZenBook models are positioned as OLED-screened, thin-and-light productivity laptops aimed at professionals, competing directly with the Dell XPS/Premium line and MacBook Air. They're sold through ASUS's own NZ storefront (asus.com/nz) as well as PB Tech, JB Hi-Fi and Noel Leeming. Expect ZenBook 14 OLED configurations to start in the $1,700-2,200 NZD range with Intel Core Ultra or Ryzen AI processors, climbing toward $2,800+ for higher-RAM, higher-storage configurations. The OLED display is the standout feature across the range and is genuinely a tier above the LCD panels in competing brands at similar price points.

ROG -- gaming laptops, and where the big money goes

ASUS ROG (Republic of Gamers) is the performance gaming line, and PB Tech's listings show real spread here -- the ROG Strix G16 sits around $2,099-2,499 NZD depending on GPU tier, while the thinner, more premium ROG Zephyrus G16 runs closer to $4,299 NZD for higher-end RTX configurations. If budget is the priority, Strix models offer most of the gaming performance of the Zephyrus line at a noticeably lower price by using a thicker, heavier chassis with a less premium finish -- a sensible trade if the laptop mostly stays on a desk.

Monitors and routers -- ASUS's quieter strengths

ASUS's monitor range (ProArt for colour-accurate creative work, TUF Gaming and ROG Swift for high refresh rate gaming panels) is comprehensively stocked at PB Tech, and it's consistently one of the better-value monitor brands in NZ for gaming panels in the 27-32 inch, 1440p, 165Hz+ range. On the networking side, ASUS routers (RT-AX and ROG Rapture Wi-Fi 6/6E lines) are a common recommendation for NZ households wanting mesh Wi-Fi upgrades over an ISP-supplied modem-router, with PB Tech carrying the broadest range of models and price points.

When to buy

Late January (back-to-school) and Boxing Day/New Year are the two most reliable discount windows across all three major NZ retailers for ASUS laptops specifically. PB Tech also runs frequent short-window "campaign price" discounts on specific SKUs year-round that are worth checking even outside the major sale periods, since they don't always align with the big seasonal sales.
