Withings vs Oura Ring: NZ Compared

Two very different approaches to health tracking that avoid the bulky smartwatch look — a hybrid analogue watch and smart scale ecosystem versus a discreet tracking ring.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026
How we picked these: Pricing checked against Noel Leeming, PB Tech and each brand's NZ store for current ScanWatch and Oura Ring models, including subscription costs where applicable.

Two different form factors

Withings builds its health ecosystem around hybrid smartwatches like the ScanWatch, which look like a normal analogue watch but pack ECG, SpO2 and sleep tracking, plus smart scales that sync body composition data automatically. The Oura Ring is a titanium ring worn on the finger, tracking sleep, readiness and activity without a screen at all.

NZ pricing

The Withings ScanWatch typically costs $449-$599 in NZ depending on size and case finish, available at Noel Leeming and PB Tech. A Withings Body smart scale adds another $99-$179. The Oura Ring costs $449-$549 upfront through Oura's NZ store, but requires an ongoing Oura membership at roughly NZ$9-10 a month to unlock full insights — a genuine ongoing cost Withings doesn't impose.

What each tracks best

  • Oura Ring: widely regarded as one of the more accurate consumer sleep and readiness trackers, thanks to consistent finger-based sensor placement
  • Withings ScanWatch: stronger for at-a-glance daily use since it's also a functioning watch, plus onboard ECG and SpO2 readings doctors can reference
  • Withings' scale ecosystem tracks body composition (weight, fat %, muscle mass) automatically, which Oura doesn't do at all
  • Both sync to their own apps, with Withings also offering broader integration into Apple Health and Google Fit

The subscription difference

This is the biggest practical decision point: Oura's full feature set is locked behind a monthly membership, so the real first-year cost is closer to $570-$670 once you add it up. Withings devices work fully without any subscription, though Withings does offer an optional Health+ subscription for deeper trend analysis that most casual users can skip entirely.

Comfort and everyday wear

The Oura Ring disappears on your finger in a way no wrist-worn device can, which matters if you dislike wearing a watch to sleep. The ScanWatch, being an actual watch, does double duty as a normal timepiece and won't look out of place in a work setting the way some fitness bands do.

Which to buy

Choose Oura Ring if sleep and recovery tracking is your main goal and you're comfortable with an ongoing subscription. Choose Withings if you want a normal-looking watch with solid health tracking and a smart scale that talks to it, with no required monthly fee.
