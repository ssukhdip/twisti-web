iPhone 18 and Apple Intelligence: What to Expect

Apple hasn't detailed iPhone 18's specific AI features yet, but Apple Intelligence itself is already real and already shipping. Here's what it does today, and a straight framework for whether it's worth upgrading for.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026

What Apple Intelligence actually is

Apple Intelligence is Apple's on-device (and partly cloud-assisted) AI system, rolled out across iOS starting with the iPhone 15 Pro and expanding to more models since. It covers writing tools (rewrite, proofread, summarise), notification summaries, Photos clean-up and search, Siri improvements, and integration with ChatGPT for more complex queries. The direction is clear even without iPhone 18 specifics: more of this running locally on the device rather than in the cloud, and deeper integration across apps rather than a separate "AI app" bolted on.

What that likely means for iPhone 18

Whatever iPhone 18 ships with, it's a safe bet that on-device AI performance keeps improving — that's been the consistent trend across recent Apple Silicon generations, and Apple has been explicit that AI is a current strategic priority. Expect faster, more capable versions of the features above, and likely expansion into more apps and system-level tasks, rather than a completely new AI paradigm arriving out of nowhere.

Worth knowing: Apple Intelligence requires a certain chip generation and enough RAM to run its on-device models — it isn't available on every iPhone still being sold. If AI features specifically matter to you, check current device compatibility before assuming any iPhone supports it.

Should you upgrade?

Upgrade if:

Your current phone doesn't support Apple Intelligence at all, is several generations old, or its battery health has genuinely degraded to the point of affecting daily use.

Keep your device if:

It already supports Apple Intelligence, performance and battery life are still solid, and you're mainly drawn in by marketing around AI features you may not use daily. Software updates typically bring the same AI feature improvements to your existing supported device, without needing new hardware.

Buying tips

  • Compare storage tiers realistically against how you actually use your phone — photos and video eat storage fast, and Apple's upgrade pricing between tiers is steep.
  • Battery life and repairability matter more over a 3+ year ownership period than benchmark numbers that rarely reflect daily use.
  • Check launch pricing against the outgoing model — last year's flagship is frequently discounted the moment a new one launches, and for most people it's still an excellent phone.
