About Twisti

One search. Every price. No guesswork.

Twisti is an AI shopping copilot for New Zealand. Give it a product, it checks every major NZ retailer at once and tells you straight: buy now, wait, or compare.

Why we built it

Comparing prices properly means opening ten tabs, remembering which store had free shipping, and hoping you didn't miss a cheaper listing somewhere else. Twisti does that legwork in seconds and gives you a plain-language verdict instead of a wall of listings.

What we do

Search a product and Twisti pulls live prices from the retailers that matter, filters out anything that looks off, and has an AI verdict weigh it up — price, alternatives, and whether now is actually a good time to buy.

Straight verdicts

Buy, wait, or compare — with the reasoning behind it, not just a price list.

No pay-to-rank

Retailers don't pay to be shown higher. What you see is what we found, sorted by price.

Price drop alerts

Track a product and we'll tell you when it actually drops — no need to keep checking.

Built in New Zealand

Made by a solo developer who got tired of tab-hopping between retailer sites to find the best deal.

Where we check prices
