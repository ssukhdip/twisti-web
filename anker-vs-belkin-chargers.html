Anker vs Belkin: Which Charger Brand to Buy

Both Anker and Belkin make solid third-party chargers and power banks, and picking between them mostly comes down to specific product lines rather than a blanket brand preference. Here's what actually differs.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026

Range and specialisation

Anker has a much broader catalogue — wall chargers, power banks, cables, car chargers, charging stations, and its own sub-brands (like Nebula for projectors and Eufy for smart home and robot vacuums). This scale means more choice at every price point, from budget basics to high-wattage GaN chargers for laptops. Belkin's range is narrower but tends to focus heavily on premium build quality and official licensing partnerships, including MagSafe-certified accessories for iPhones made in partnership with Apple.

If you specifically want a MagSafe charger that's officially Apple-certified, Belkin has historically had a stronger presence there. For general-purpose fast chargers and power banks across a wide range of price points, Anker's catalogue gives you more options to match your exact wattage and port needs.

GaN charger technology

Both brands have embraced GaN (gallium nitride) chargers, which run cooler and pack more power into a smaller housing than older silicon-based chargers. Anker was an early and aggressive adopter of GaN tech across its range, often at competitive prices. Belkin also offers GaN chargers, generally at a modest premium, positioned as part of its higher-end BoostCharge Pro line.

Build quality and reliability

Both brands are generally well regarded for reliability compared to unbranded or generic third-party chargers, and both offer connected-equipment or device-protection warranties on many products (worth checking the specific terms and coverage amount for the product you're buying, as these vary by line and region). Anker's higher unit volumes mean there's more user review data available for most of its popular products, while Belkin's smaller range tends to be more consistently premium-positioned.

  • Power banks: Anker has a much wider range of capacities and form factors to choose from.
  • Wall chargers: both brands offer multi-port GaN options; compare wattage and port count for your specific devices.
  • MagSafe/wireless: Belkin has stronger official Apple partnership credentials here.
Tip: When comparing power banks, check the actual usable capacity rather than the rated mAh figure alone — real-world output is always somewhat lower due to conversion losses, and this applies to both brands equally.

Price in NZ

Anker generally undercuts Belkin at equivalent specs, particularly for power banks and basic wall chargers, reflecting its broader manufacturing scale. Belkin's premium positioning, especially for MagSafe and officially licensed accessories, tends to command a higher price for comparable charging speed. Neither brand is dramatically cheaper across the board — it really depends on the specific product category and current retailer pricing.
