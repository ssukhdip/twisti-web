Dyson Airwrap in NZ: models, attachments and buying tips

The Airwrap is one of Dyson's priciest personal care products, and the lineup includes several variants with different attachment sets. Here's how to work out which one is worth it.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026

What the Airwrap actually does

The Airwrap styles hair using controlled airflow (the Coanda effect) to curl, wave, smooth or dry hair without extreme heat, using interchangeable barrels and brush attachments rather than a single fixed styling head. It's positioned as a lower-heat-damage alternative to traditional curling irons and straighteners, which is the core of its appeal, alongside the styling versatility multiple attachments provide.

The lineup varies by attachment set

Dyson has sold the Airwrap in several configurations over time, generally differentiated by which barrels and brushes are included — some sets are aimed at long hair, others at shorter styles or a broader range of curl types. Long-barrel sets typically add attachments suited to longer hair lengths, while complete or "multi-styler" sets bundle a wider range of tools for both curling and smoothing at a higher price point.

Full set vs starter set

A smaller attachment set is cheaper and covers the basics — usually a couple of curling barrels and a smoothing brush. The larger, more complete sets add extra barrel sizes and additional round or paddle brushes for smoothing and volumising, at a noticeably higher price. If you're new to the product and unsure you'll use every attachment, starting with a smaller set and checking whether you actually miss the extras is a reasonable approach — extra attachments can sometimes be bought separately later.

What drives the price in NZ

Airwrap pricing in New Zealand sits well above most competing hair stylers, reflecting Dyson's positioning and the multi-attachment engineering involved. Within the range, price differences mostly track the number and type of attachments included rather than any difference in the core motor or handle. Colour/limited editions occasionally carry a small premium over standard colourways too.

Where to buy and what to watch for

Dyson sells directly through its NZ website, and the Airwrap is also stocked by major retailers including Noel Leeming, Harvey Norman, Farmers and some appliance specialists. Because it's a popular gifting item, prices can move around key retail periods like Mother's Day and Black Friday — worth checking a price history rather than buying on the first listing you see.

Watch for parallel-imported units: Some third-party sellers list Airwrap models imported from other regions, which may use different plug types or voltage specifications not suited to NZ power outlets, and may not be covered by Dyson NZ's local warranty. Buying from an authorised NZ retailer avoids this risk.
