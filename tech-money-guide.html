The Ultimate Guide to Saving Money with Technology

Most "save money with tech" advice is vague. This is the specific, practical version — with links to a full guide on each area.

Twisti guides · Updated July 2026

The pattern across all of it: the technology that actually saves money is the technology that removes waste you weren't tracking — standby power, forgotten subscriptions, and price guesswork. None of it requires buying something new to "save" money; most of it is habits and a bit of automation.
